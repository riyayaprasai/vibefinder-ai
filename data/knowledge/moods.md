# Mood notes

Each section is one mood. `related` moods earn partial credit. `profile` gives default targets
that are only used when the listener did not state them.

## happy
aliases: happy, cheerful, joyful, feel good, feel-good, uplifting
related: playful, euphoric
profile: valence=0.85
Happy songs are bright, positive and uplifting, with major keys and a bouncy feel that lifts your mood.

## chill
aliases: chill, chilled, mellow, laid back, laid-back, low key
related: relaxed, focused
profile: energy=0.35, valence=0.6
Chill music is calm and unhurried. It keeps a soft, steady groove without demanding attention.

## relaxed
aliases: relaxed, relaxing, easygoing, soothing
related: chill, romantic
profile: energy=0.35, valence=0.7
Relaxed songs are gentle and warm, ideal for unwinding at the end of the day.

## focused
aliases: focused, focus, concentrate, concentration
related: chill
profile: energy=0.4, instrumentalness=0.85
Focused music is steady, repetitive and mostly wordless so it can sit in the background while you concentrate.

## intense
aliases: intense, hype, pumped, fired up
related: aggressive, euphoric
profile: energy=0.9
Intense music is loud, fast and driving. It builds tension and adrenaline.

## aggressive
aliases: aggressive, angry, rage, furious, heavy
related: intense
profile: energy=0.95, valence=0.3
Aggressive songs are harsh, dark and powerful, built to release anger and frustration.

## euphoric
aliases: euphoric, ecstatic, blissful
related: happy, intense
profile: energy=0.9, valence=0.8
Euphoric music peaks with big drops and soaring synths, the feeling of a festival crowd at its height.

## playful
aliases: playful, fun, silly, bouncy
related: happy
profile: valence=0.8
Playful songs are fun, bold and confident, with bouncy rhythms that make you move.

## romantic
aliases: romantic, romance, love songs, sensual
related: relaxed
profile: energy=0.45, valence=0.65
Romantic music is smooth, warm and intimate, the kind of song for a slow dance or candlelit dinner.

## moody
aliases: moody, brooding, dark and moody
related: nostalgic, mysterious
profile: valence=0.45
Moody songs are nocturnal and introspective, sitting between sad and calm.

## nostalgic
aliases: nostalgic, nostalgia, throwback, retro
related: moody
profile: valence=0.6
Nostalgic music sounds like memories: warm, wistful and a little bittersweet.

## melancholy
aliases: melancholy, melancholic, sad, sorrowful, heartbroken, gloomy, depressing
related: moody
profile: valence=0.25, energy=0.3
Melancholy music is slow, sad and lonely, often in minor keys. It is for rainy nights and heartbreak.

## mysterious
aliases: mysterious, eerie, enigmatic, haunting
related: moody
profile: valence=0.4
Mysterious music is spacious and unresolved, with exotic scales and a sense of distance.
