# Genre notes

Hand-written notes for VibeFinder 2.0. Each section is one retrievable document.
Fields: `aliases` (words a listener might type), `related` (genres that deserve partial credit).
The paragraph under the fields is the text that the index searches and that explanations quote.

## pop
aliases: pop, pop music, top 40, chart pop
related: indie pop, latin, electronic
Pop is built around bright hooks, polished production and choruses meant to be sung along with. It is usually upbeat, mid to fast tempo and made for radio, parties and everyday listening.

## indie pop
aliases: indie pop, indie, bedroom pop
related: pop, synthwave
Indie pop keeps the catchy melodies of pop but with a lighter, homemade or nostalgic feel. It often sounds sunny and warm rather than slick.

## lofi
aliases: lofi, lo-fi, lo fi, chillhop, lofi hip hop, study beats
related: ambient, jazz, hip hop
Lofi is soft, dusty, beat-driven background music with warm crackle and simple loops. It is slow, calm and mostly instrumental, which is why people play it while studying, reading or working late.

## ambient
aliases: ambient, soundscape, soundscapes, drone
related: lofi, classical, world
Ambient music is spacious and atmospheric, with slow-moving textures and little rhythm. It feels dreamy and floating, good for sleep, meditation and deep focus.

## jazz
aliases: jazz, smooth jazz, jazzy
related: lofi, r&b, classical
Jazz in this catalog is warm, acoustic and relaxed, with brushed drums, piano and upright bass. It fits coffee shops, slow mornings and quiet dinners.

## synthwave
aliases: synthwave, synth, synths, retrowave, outrun
related: electronic, indie pop
Synthwave uses analog synthesizers and drum machines to sound like a retro 1980s night drive. It is moody, nocturnal and nostalgic, with a steady driving pulse.

## rock
aliases: rock, rock music, guitar music, alt rock
related: metal, indie pop
Rock is guitar-driven, loud and intense, with live drums and a strong backbeat. It is powerful and driving, good for workouts and blowing off steam.

## metal
aliases: metal, heavy metal, metalcore
related: rock
Metal is the heaviest, most aggressive end of guitar music: distorted riffs, fast double kick drums and dark, powerful energy.

## hip hop
aliases: hip hop, hip-hop, rap, trap
related: r&b, pop, lofi
Hip hop centers on rhythm and vocal flow over beats. This catalog's hip hop is playful, bold and very danceable, made for parties.

## r&b
aliases: r&b, rnb, r and b, soul, neo soul
related: hip hop, jazz, pop
R&B is smooth, warm and intimate, with soulful vocals and a relaxed groove. It suits romantic evenings and slow late-night listening.

## electronic
aliases: electronic, edm, dance music, house, techno, rave
related: pop, synthwave
Electronic dance music is built from synthesizers and pounding four-on-the-floor beats. It is euphoric, high energy and made for clubs, festivals and dancing.

## country
aliases: country, country music, americana, folk
related: rock, indie pop
Country tells stories over acoustic guitar, fiddle and steady rhythms. It feels nostalgic and warm, like a road trip down a dusty highway.

## classical
aliases: classical, orchestral, orchestra, piano music, symphony
related: ambient, jazz
Classical music here means slow acoustic orchestral and piano pieces. It is fully instrumental and can be calm, emotional or melancholic.

## latin
aliases: latin, reggaeton, salsa, latin pop
related: pop, hip hop
Latin pop blends Caribbean and Latin American rhythms with pop hooks. It is sunny, playful and extremely danceable.

## world
aliases: world, world music, global
related: ambient, classical
World music in this catalog is atmospheric and mysterious, using traditional instruments and spacious, desert-like textures.

## k-pop
aliases: k-pop, kpop, korean pop
related: pop, electronic, hip hop
K-pop is Korean pop music with high-energy choreography, glossy production and a mix of pop, electronic and hip hop styles.

## punk
aliases: punk, punk rock, pop punk
related: rock, metal
Punk is short, fast, loud and raw guitar music with a rebellious attitude.

## reggae
aliases: reggae, dub, ska
related: latin, hip hop
Reggae is laid-back Jamaican music with an off-beat guitar skank and a heavy bass groove.
